Kid Apps

Little browser toys. No ads, no accounts, nothing to install — and they keep working with the wifi off.

🐱 Talking Cat Say something and the cat says it back in a silly voice. Poke it to see what it does. 🧩 Puzzle Play Drag the pieces into the frame and the picture appears. 4 to 25 pieces, ages 3 to 10.

Both are hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step, no dependencies, and they run on an E Ink tablet as happily as on a phone.
